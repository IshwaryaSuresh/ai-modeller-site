A SketchUp-grade modeller for architecture, interior and landscape practices. It reads your working drawings and builds the model. You make the small edits, and it turns the model back into drawings.

Reads DWG, DXF, PDF and sketches Every change previewed before it lands Runs in the browser on an everyday laptop

The problem

Studios build the same building three times

1 · Drawings

Working drawings in AutoCAD: plans, sections and elevations.

2 · Model

Rebuilt by hand in SketchUp or Revit so the client can see it.

3 · Visuals

Exported again to a separate renderer, often on a GPU workstation.

Every design change means redrawing by hand. The drawings and the model drift apart, and mistakes on site start there. Pretty images are a solved problem. A model the studio can trust is not.

How it works

One chain, in both directions

01

Drop your plan

DWG or DXF from CAD, a PDF set, or a photo of a hand sketch. There is no blank canvas to start from.

02

It reads the drawings

Walls, openings, columns, rooms and levels are read across the drawings together.

03

You confirm

It shows the plinth, sill and lintel levels it found and flags where the drawings disagree. Nothing is built until you confirm.

04

Edit, then export

Click a part or type a change. Plans, sections and elevations update together, ready to export.

Plan reading

Nothing locks until the architect confirms

A plan does not carry heights, so the model asks for them. It shows the levels it read from the drawings and lets you correct each one before it builds the house.

  • Rooms, walls, doors and windows counted as they are read
  • Levels taken from the plan, or from Tamil Nadu residential defaults
  • Every value can be changed later from the building's panel

The AI as a colleague

Point at it, say what you want

Select the verandah columns and say "make it an archway". The request applies only to what you selected. The AI edits the model's description, not its geometry, so every change is a named step you can undo.

  • Every proposal is shown in the model first. New and changed parts are highlighted, and removed parts appear in red.
  • Each proposal states its reason and the references it used, and waits for Accept or Reject
  • When something is unclear, the AI asks you before it guesses

Rules, not meshes

Anything that repeats is a unit and a pattern

Jalis, brick bonds, cladding, louvres, pavers and rows of trees all run on one modular engine. Change the unit, the region or the bond and it tiles again at once, with whole and cut units counted.

  • Regions are tied to columns and levels: a jali set "sill to lintel" stays there when the sill moves
  • Custom units from a photo, a product sheet, a sketch or a line of text
  • Save a unit to your library and use it again on any project

Drawings out

One change, every sheet updated

Plans, elevations and sections are drawn from the model, never by hand. Raise the plinth and export again, and every sheet follows.

  • PDF sheets and CAD (DXF) in mm, cm, m or feet and inches, at 1:20 to 1:500, on A4 to A0
  • One CAD layer per element type, with line weights set by type
  • Quantities as CSV, and IFC for BIM, plus OBJ, glTF, STL and USDZ

The moment that sells it

Show it a reference. It builds it into your model.

Mark a zone, drop in an image from Pinterest, a site photo or a product sheet, and get real fins, jali, screens or a whole fitted wall. The widths, spacing and unit counts are all editable, and they export straight to drawings.

The whole studio, in one model

Three modes: Model, Landscape and Present

Model

The full SketchUp tool set

Push/Pull, Follow Me, Offset, solid tools, sections, scenes, styles, tags, guides, Match Photo and a measurements box. Everyone gets the full set, and the tools appear only when they are needed.

Landscape

A native plant agent

Give it the site and it researches the native plants for that place on the web. It returns sizes, growth, water and sun needs, local names, and a source for every fact. Plant one at a time or in rows, then slide through the years to watch the garden grow.

Present

For the client

Every tool is hidden. Share live links that follow each edit, show the house life-size in VR or as a 1:50 model in AR, and print presentation sheets with a title block.

Who it's for

Architecture, interior and landscape practices

From one-person studios to large firms. It starts in Tamil Nadu, with Indian building conventions built in: plinths, sills and lintel bands, chajjas, jalis, thinnais and tulsi maadams.

  • Drawing-first studios upload their DWG and get the model, with a confirm step for every level.
  • Model-first studios design in the model and get the drawings out.
  • Homeowners working with an architect can understand the house and change it in plain words.
  • Everyday hardware. It runs in the browser, with no GPU workstation needed. Booleans run on the open-source Manifold engine.
